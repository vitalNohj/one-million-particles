2 | -----------

/CONTRIBUTING.md:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1 | Contributing
 2 | ============
 3 | 
 4 | Please follow the existing style of the project, including variable declaration placement, prototype property declarations, etc.
 5 | 
 6 | As a short guide:
 7 | 
 8 | * **Indentation**: 4 spaces.
 9 | * **Whitespace**: No trailing whitespace.
10 | * **JSHint**: Run JSHint. A `.jshintrc` is included.
11 | * **JSBeautify** An included `.jsbeautifyrc` will ensure your PR is as close to the style of this library as possible. Do use this.
12 | * **Description**: Please provide a full description of your changes.
13 | * **Comments**: Follow existing commenting conventions.
14 | * **Parentheses**: 1 space before and after parentheses.

 2 | (function() {
 3 |     var source = document.getElementsByClassName('prettyprint source linenums');
 4 |     var i = 0;
 5 |     var lineNumber = 0;
 6 |     var lineId;
 7 |     var lines;
 8 |     var totalLines;
 9 |     var anchorHash;
10 | 
11 |     if (source && source[0]) {
12 |         anchorHash = document.location.hash.substring(1);
13 |         lines = source[0].getElementsByTagName('li');
14 |         totalLines = lines.length;
15 | 
16 |         for (; i < totalLines; i++) {
17 |             lineNumber++;
18 |             lineId = 'line' + lineNumber;
19 |             lines[i].id = lineId;
20 |             if (lineId === anchorHash) {
21 |                 lines[i].className += ' selected';
22 |             }
23 |         }
24 |     }
25 | })();

 1 | SPE.Group
 2 | =========
 3 | 
 4 | 
 5 | Creating a group
 6 | ----------------
 7 | A group is created as follows, and takes a map of option key/value pairs as its only argument:
 8 | 
 9 | ```
10 | var group = new SPE.Group( {
11 | 	optionName: optionValue,
12 | 	// etc.
13 | } );
14 | ```
15 | 
16 | Adding emitters to a group
17 | --------------------------
18 | Many emitters can be added to a single group. These emitters will be rendered using the same attribute buffers and will save valuable time in your render loop by keeping CPU -> GPU data transfer to a minimum.
19 | 
20 | To add an emitter to a group, do the following, where `emitter` is an instance of `SPE.Emitter`.
21 | 
22 | ```
23 | group.addEmitter( emitter );
24 | ```
25 | 
26 | If you have multiple emitters to add at the same time, the method can be 'chained' as follows:
27 | ```
28 | group.addEmitter( emitter1 ).addEmitter( emitter2 ); // etc.
29 | ```
30 | 
31 | 
32 | Adding a group to a scene
33 | -------------------------
34 | To render the group, add the group's `mesh` object to your scene:
35 | 
36 | ```
37 | scene.add( group.mesh );
38 | ```
39 | 
40 | 
41 | Updating a group
42 | ----------------
43 | A group must be updated on a per-frame basis, so within a render/animation loop do the following to update the group:
44 | 
45 | ```
46 | group.tick( deltaTime );
47 | ```
48 | 
49 | The deltaTime argument is a number denoting the time it took to render the previous frame. It's easily got by using an instance of `THREE.Clock()` and using the `.getDelta()` method. If you don't pass a `deltaTime` argument to your group's `.tick()` function, the value of `group.fixedTimeStep` will be used.
50 | 
51 | 
52 | 
53 | Configuring a group
54 | -------------------
55 | See the [API documentation](https://squarefeet.github.io/ShaderParticleEngine/docs/api/global.html#GroupOptions)

/docs/MigrationLog.md:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1 | Migration Log
 2 | =============
 3 | 
 4 | #### Emitter types are now "constants" and reside in SPE.distributions:
 5 | 
 6 | When making an emitter and setting its `type` property, you must now
 7 | reference one of the `SPE.distributions` "constants" instead of passing
 8 | a string value ('sphere', 'cube', etc.) as in previous versions.
 9 | 
10 | Constants are as follows:
11 | * `SPE.distributions.SPHERE`
12 | * `SPE.distributions.DISC` (note the spelling has changed from 'disk'!)
13 | * `SPE.distributions.BOX` (no longer 'cube')
14 | 
15 | **Example**
16 | ```javascript
17 | var emitter = new SPE.Emitter( {
18 | 	type: SPE.distributions.SPHERE
19 | } );
20 | ```
21 | 
22 | 
23 | 
24 | #### MaxAge property is now part of `SPE.Emitter` properties
25 | 
26 | `SPE.Group` no longer takes a `maxAge` property. Maximum ages for particles
27 | are calculated on a per-particle basis.
28 | 
29 | **Example**
30 | ```javascript
31 | var emitter = new SPE.Emitter( {
32 | 	// Gives a maxAge range of 3 to 7
33 | 	maxAge: {
34 | 		value: 5,
35 | 		spread: 2
36 | 	}
37 | } );
38 | ```
39 | 
40 | 
41 | #### `SPE.Emitter.alive` is no longer a numeric value ####
42 | The `alive` property of an `SPE.Emitter` instance is now a Boolean. The previous functionality of using the `alive` property as a value to determine what percentage of an emitter's particles were alive has been moved to the `activeMultiplier` property.
43 | 
44 | The benefit here is that you get a more fine-grained control over the number of particles emitter per-second for an emitter. Values greater than `1` will emulate a burst of particles, causing the emitter to run out of particles before it's next activation cycle, and values less than `1` and greater than `0` will emitter fewer particles per second than the emitter's default.
45 | 
46 | 
47 | ### Texture property supports sprite sheets ###
48 | The `texture` property of `SPE.Group` is in a slightly different format, but now supports animated sprite sheets:
49 | 
50 | **Example**
51 | ```javascript
52 | var group = new SPE.Group( {
53 | 	texture: {
54 | 		value: THREE.ImageUtils.loadTexture( ... ),
55 | 		frames: new THREE.Vector2( 4, 4 ), // Optional. No. frames on x/y axis of texture
56 | 		frameCount: 16, // Optional. If whole texture isn't used, specify the total number of frames the texture has here
57 | 		loop: 2, // Optional. The number of loops the spritesheet should perform during a particle's lifetime.
58 | 	}
59 | } );

/docs/SPE.Emitter.md:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1 | SPE.Emitter
 2 | ===========
 3 | 
 4 | Creating an Emitter
 5 | -------------------
 6 | A single particle emitter is created as shown below. It's only argument is a map of [options](#configuring-an-emitter):
 7 | 
 8 | ```javascript
 9 | var emitter = new SPE.Emitter( {
10 | 	optionName: optionValue
11 | } );
12 | ```
13 | 
14 | 
15 | Adding an Emitter to a Group
16 | ----------------------------
17 | Many emitters can be added to a single group. These emitters will be rendered using the same attribute buffers and will save valuable time in your render loop by keeping CPU -> GPU data transfer to a minimum.
18 | 
19 | To add an emitter to a group, do the following:
20 | 
21 | ```javascript
22 | group.addEmitter( emitter );
23 | ```
24 | 
25 | If you have multiple emitters to add at the same time, the method can be 'chained' as follows:
26 | 
27 | ```javascript
28 | group.addEmitter( emitter1 ).addEmitter( emitter2 ); // etc.
29 | ```
30 | 
31 | 
32 | Removing an Emitter from a Group
33 | --------------------------------
34 | Emitters can also be removed from a group, but bear in mind that in doing so, the emitter will be instantly removed from the scene. Any particles that were onscreen at the time of removal will disappear.
35 | 
36 | If you need to just turn off an emitter, use the emitter's `disable()` function instead.
37 | 
38 | To remove an emitter from a group:
39 | 
40 | ```javascript
41 | // Either:
42 | group.removeEmitter( emitter);
43 | 
44 | // Or:
45 | emitter.remove();
46 | ```
47 | 
48 | 
49 | 
50 | 
51 | Configuring an Emitter
52 | ----------------------
53 | An emitter's properties can be split into three groups:
54 | 
55 | 
56 | * Basic properties
57 | * Properties that can change over a particle's lifetime.
58 | * General configuration.
59 | 
60 | Basic properties constists of `position`, `velocity`, `acceleration`, `radius` (only used when `SPE.distributions.DISC` or `SPE.distributions.SPHERE` is used), `drag`, `wiggle`, `rotation`, and `maxAge`. These properties do _not_ change over the course of a particle's lifetime, but most of them _can_ be re-randomised when a particle is spawned to avoid consistent behaviours.
61 | 
62 | Properties that can change over a particle's lifetime are referred to as _value-over-lifetime_ properties. These are: `color`, `size`, `opacity`, and `angle` (read: _texture rotation_). These properties can either be given an array of values that a particle will move through over its lifetime, or just a single value if a lifetime-relative value isn't required.
63 | 
64 | Most of the above properties can be randomised through use of a `spread` property.
65 | 
66 | The general configuration properties consist of `duration`, `isStatic`, `particleCount`, and `activeMultiplier`.
67 | 
68 | For more information on an emitter's properties and available values and types, see the [API documentation](https://squarefeet.github.io/ShaderParticleEngine/docs/api/global.html#EmitterOptions)

 1 | Shader Particle Engine v1.0.6 ![](https://travis-ci.org/squarefeet/ShaderParticleEngine.svg?branch=master)
 2 | =============================
 3 | 
 4 | 
 5 | Overview
 6 | --------
 7 | The purpose of this library is to make creating particle effects using THREE.js and WebGL as simple as possible. The heavy-lifting is done by the GPU, freeing up CPU cycles.
 8 | 
 9 | Emitters are created by first creating an instance of `SPE.Group`. It is in the group where `ShaderMaterial` settings are applied, and the texture for all emitters added to that group is set. Multiple groups can be created, but if efficiency is a high-priority then as few groups as possible should be created. The group takes care of uploading emitter data to the GPU for simulation and rendering, so the fewer chunks of data that get sent the better.
10 | 
11 | Once a group has been created, an instance of `SPE.Emitter` can then be added to it. Each emitter can have its own behaviour and appearance.
12 | 
13 | 
14 | For more information on groups and emitters, see the docs below:
15 | 
16 | * [Group documentation](./docs/SPE.Group.md)
17 | * [Emitter documentation](./docs/SPE.Emitter.md)
18 | 
19 | 
20 | Examples can be found [here](https://squarefeet.github.io/ShaderParticleEngine/)
21 | 
22 | 
23 |

25 | ------------
26 | * THREE.js r72+ (preferably r81+).
